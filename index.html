Questions

Do I really pay nothing until I see the site?

Yes. We build it first and send you a link. You only get an invoice if you decide you want it live.

I already have a website. Can you still help?

Yes. Most of our sites replace an older one. We'll build the new version alongside it, and you can compare them side by side before switching.

Do I need to buy a domain or hosting?

If you already have a domain, we'll use it. If not, we'll help you pick one. Hosting is included.

Who owns the site?

You do. If you ever leave, we'll hand over everything.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer through a secure invoice, once you've approved your site.
